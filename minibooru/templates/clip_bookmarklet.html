“Save to {{ brand.name }}” bookmark

For sites where you are logged in: while you look at a post, click the bookmark. A small {{ brand.name }} window opens with the post's pictures, title and tags; check them and press Save.

The files are downloaded by your own browser with your own login, like viewing the post. Nothing logs in for you, nothing crawls the site, and your password never reaches {{ brand.name }}.

1. Add the bookmark

Drag this button to your bookmarks bar (Brave/Chrome: press Ctrl+Shift+B if the bar is hidden):

📥 Save to {{ brand.name }}

Can't drag? Make it by hand

Create a new bookmark, name it “📥 Save to {{ brand.name }}” (the emoji shows as its icon), and paste this as its URL:

Or: a toolbar button with {{ brand.name }}'s icon

Bookmarks can't show a picture as their icon, but a small browser button can. It sits next to the address bar, shows the app's icon and does the same job. Brave, Chrome and Edge:

  1. Download the button and unzip it to a folder you keep (the browser loads it from there).
  2. Open brave://extensions (Chrome: chrome://extensions, Edge: edge://extensions) and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Press Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder.
  4. Click the puzzle-piece icon by the address bar and pin Save to {{ brand.name }}.

It can only see the tab you click it on, at that moment. If you change the app's icon, name or address, download it again and press ↻ on its card on the extensions page.

2. Use it

  1. Open the post on the site (logged in as usual).
  2. Click Save to {{ brand.name }} (bookmark or button). If the browser blocks the window, click the blue bubble that appears on the page.
  3. In the window: check the files and tags (remove or add any), then Save.

This bookmark is made for . If you open {{ brand.name }} under a different address (for example the https one vs the home-network one), get the bookmark again from that address.

Nothing happens when you click the bookmark? Some sites block bookmarks like this one; use the toolbar button above instead.

On iPhone: this bookmark doesn't work there (iPhones only let a full App Store app download pictures that a site protects). Save those posts from a computer.
